# The Old Groves — Celtic Paganism

A Crusader Kings III mod that brings back the old Celtic faith: **Druidism**, with its own gods, holy sites, doctrines and vocabulary, and a quest to found it again.

This is alternate history: no one revived the Celtic faith in the ninth century. But every piece of it is built with care for credibility: the gods, rites, doctrines and holy sites come from ancient authors, Irish and Welsh literature, inscriptions and archaeology.

**Version:** 1.0 · **Game version:** 1.20 · **Languages:** English, French, German, Spanish

## How to play it

The faith starts **dead**: in 867 nobody follows it. Three ways to bring it back:

- **Dubgall the Apostate** (bookmark, requires *Roads to Power*): a former monk of Iona turned adventurer, and the full restoration quest.
- **The Old Groves** (bookmark, no DLC needed): **Rhodri Mawr** of Gwynedd, **Dumnarth** of Cornwall or **Salomon** of Brittany, who can turn back to the old gods with a decision.
- **The Ruler Designer**: choose Druidism as the faith. A landed character works with the base game; a landless adventurer needs *Roads to Power*.

Achievements are disabled while a mod is active.

## What's in this version

**The restoration quest.** Dubgall finds a carved stone in the heather of Skye and sets out to understand it. Steal, buy or beg the books of five monasteries, visit the sacred places of the old gods, learn the prayers from the last keeper of a hidden cult, dig up a golden sickle in Armorica and a war horn at Alesia, perform the first rite on Ynys Môn, write the **Book of the Druids** and swear the oath of Beltaine. Then win lords and people to the faith, and end a cattle plague with the fires of Beltaine. Companions join you along the way, each with their own story; the quest passes to your heir if you die. Follow it in the **Situations** window.

**After the quest:** the casus belli **Return the Land to the Old Gods** against the Christians of Britain and Ireland, the **Spear of Lug**, and armies of Lug's faithful.

**Decisions for every Celtic ruler:** **Renew the Bond with the Old Gods** (Goidelic and Brythonic rulers, while the faith is dead) and **Take Up the Heritage of the Groves** (the casus belli and the Spear of Lug for druid dynasties that did not do the quest).

**Eight artefacts and relics**, with their own icons: the golden sickle, the Gaulish carnyx, the mistletoe crown of Môn, the Book of the Druids, the Spear of Lug, the cauldron of the Dagda, the silver branch and the torc of Lugus. The last ones are found on pilgrimage to the holy sites.

**The tent of offerings**, a camp building given by the faithful to an adventurer founder, which grows after the miracle.

**One religion, one faith, four rites.** Each rite has its own name, gods, tenets and customs:

| Rite | People | Tenets |
|---|---|---|
| **Druídecht** | Gaelic | Reincarnation, Ritual Celebrations, Ancestor Worship |
| **Plant Dôn** | Brythonic | Reincarnation, Megalithic Constructions, Sanctity of Nature |
| **Gutuatrism** | Gaulish | Reincarnation, Cranial Trophies, Astrology |
| **Lugovism** | Celtiberian | Reincarnation, Ritual Hospitality, Cranial Trophies |

**A real pantheon.** Every god slot is filled with a god from the sources, rite by rite: Lug, Dagda, Brigit and the Morrígan for the Gaels; Lleu, Gwydion, Arianrhod and Ceridwen for the Britons; Lugus, Sucellos, Toutatis and Cernunnos for the Gauls. Lug, under his three names (Lug, Lleu, Lugus), is the high god of every rite: he is the only god attested in all four branches.

**Six holy sites**, each with its own effect: **Tara**, **Ynys Môn** and the **Forest of the Carnutes** (the three eminent sites), **Iona** (shared with the Christians), **Peñalba de Villastar** and **Alesia**.

**The Archdruidate**: once the faith is reformed, its head takes the title of Archdruid, with its own coat of arms.

**Doctrines drawn from the sources**, including a new one: **Judges and Arbiters**. Caesar writes that the druids judged crimes, inheritances and boundary disputes; with this doctrine, punishment comes from a druidic sentence and rulers gain less tyranny.

**Virtues and sins** of the Celtic world: brave, generous, just and **poet** (the Irish *filid*, the Welsh bards); cowardly, greedy and arbitrary.

**Its own vocabulary**: druids and druidesses, the *nemeton*, the Book of the Druids, the Otherworld.

## Requirements

No DLC is required to play the faith or the Old Groves bookmark. Optional:

- *Roads to Power* for the Dubgall bookmark and to play a landless adventurer
- *All Under Heaven* for the Cranial Trophies tenet (Gaulish and Celtiberian rites)
- *Legends of the Dead* for the legend seed given at the founding

## Compatibility

The mod only adds new files and overwrites no vanilla file. It should work with any mod that doesn't rewrite the pagan religions.
